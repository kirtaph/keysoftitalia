# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Local residents, students, professionals, and small businesses in Ginosa and surrounding areas. These users are seeking reliable technical repairs for their devices (smartphones, PCs, tablets) or affordable, high-quality refurbished devices. Their main concern is finding a trustworthy partner who won't overcharge, will handle their data securely, and can deliver fast turnaround times.

## Product Purpose

The primary purpose of the website is to market Key Soft Italia's repair services, generate customer leads through online assistance and quote request forms, and showcase their catalog of certified refurbished tech products. Success means turning website visitors into online service requests, direct WhatsApp chats, or physical store visits.

## Positioning

The trusted local reference for fast, certified, and professional tech repairs and refurbished devices since 2004.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: The primary Call to Action is submitting a repair assistance or quote request form online. The secondary Call to Action is starting a direct chat via a pre-filled WhatsApp link.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Fast, reliable tech repairs and certified refurbished devices close to you, backed by 20+ years of trust.
- Belief ladder:
  1. Key Soft Italia is an established, physical store in Ginosa with over two decades of local reputation.
  2. Their repair services cover my specific device category with high competence and speed.
  3. Their refurbished products are fully certified, thoroughly tested, and sold with a warranty, making them a safe alternative to new devices.
  4. Getting a quote or reaching out via WhatsApp is friction-free and gets me a quick response.
- Proof on hand: Over 20 years of continuous service in the Ginosa community, local physical storefront address, warranty guarantees on all refurbished sales, and built-in WhatsApp direct line.

## Brand Personality

Professional, trusted, and close. The brand is deeply knowledgeable and technically expert, yet remains friendly, accessible, and community-minded rather than corporate or distant.

## Anti-references

- Generic Bootstrap templates with default styling, which feel cheap, generic, or outdated.
- "Shady tech repair" aesthetics with neon colors, cluttered pages, flashing text, or poorly sized icons.
- Cold, hyper-minimalist tech-agency styles that look sleek to designers but feel intimidating or confusing to everyday local consumers.

## Design Principles

- **Local Authority Through Professionalism**: Elevate the local service by presenting a polished, clean, and high-quality interface that rivals national tech support brands, showing that Ginosa has top-tier expertise right down the street.
- **Accessible Expert Reassurance**: Explain services and processes in plain, friendly language while maintaining high visual order to build reassurance.
- **Frictionless Action Pathways**: Keep contact channels, quote forms, and WhatsApp access prominent, clear, and extremely easy to use at any stage of the user journey.
- **Human Connection and Trust**: Weave Key Soft's long-standing local history (since 2004) and physical presence into the typography, content rhythm, and messaging, avoiding clinical, stock-only corporate vibes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all pages.
- Ensure high color contrast (at least 4.5:1 ratio) for all body text, placeholders, and interactive elements.
- Maintain a clear heading hierarchy (H1 -> H2 -> H3) and semantic HTML for screen readers.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion media queries by offering alternative crossfades or static transitions for animations.
