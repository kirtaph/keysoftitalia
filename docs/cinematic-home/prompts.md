# Prompt di produzione

## 1 — Frame iniziale

Create a photorealistic premium product-film keyframe, landscape 16:9, minimum
1920x1080. One unbranded graphite smartphone with physically credible aluminum
edges and a black glass front, a subtle branching crack visible across the glass.
Three-quarter front angle, standing diagonally, occupying the center-right 45%
of the image. Near-black seamless studio environment. Warm amber-orange rim
light inspired by #ff6b35, soft large white studio reflections on glass and metal,
restrained contrast, tangible realistic material microdetail. The left 40% is
quiet negative space for later HTML typography. No floating accessories, no
particles, no rings, no neon grid, no holograms. Keep the subject inside the
central 65% so a vertical crop can preserve the action. No text, no letters,
no logo, no watermark, no hands, no people. This is an opening film frame,
not an advertisement layout. A calm close camera with a 65mm product lens.

## 2 — Frame finale (editing, con il primo frame come riferimento)

Use the supplied starting keyframe as the authoritative identity reference.
Preserve exactly the same unbranded graphite smartphone geometry, corner radii,
aluminum frame thickness, camera configuration, studio background and lighting
identity. Show the same device repaired and fully assembled: clean intact glass,
screen lit with a simple abstract warm orange light field, no UI or text. Camera
slightly farther away and smartphone rotated just enough to reveal its metal
edge. Subject remains center-right and fully inside a safe central crop.
Maintain physically credible glass and metal reflections. No new device,
accessories, written branding, humans, particles or interface labels.

## 3 — Film continuo, 12 secondi

Create one continuous 12-second photorealistic product film connecting the
provided first and final frames. The exact same single graphite smartphone
must persist with consistent geometry, scale relationships, lighting and
materials. There are no cuts or camera jumps. Scene is a seamless near-black
studio with restrained amber-orange rim light and soft white glass reflections.

0–2 seconds: slowly approach the cracked front glass, gently changing angle
so its fissure catches the light. Keep the phone center-right and leave clean
negative space on the left. The device must not morph or drift to an edge.

2–4 seconds: rotate the same device smoothly and open it into exactly three
physically credible aligned layers: glass display, internal components, rear
housing. The surroundings recede into black. The device alone carries the
frame. Do not show a second environment or another phone. This isolated
transition must contain usable continuous motion rather than an abrupt reveal.

4–8 seconds: move the camera closer to the internal circuit board and battery,
keeping the layered device identity evident. Soft amber light reveals material
detail. A clean intact display layer returns toward the housing and the three
layers align, reassembling with believable mechanical spacing. No tools or
hands and no tutorial annotations. Preserve frame shape and component layout.

8–12 seconds: pull back smoothly to the repaired device, now fully assembled.
Its screen powers on with an abstract orange light field without text or UI.
Settle into the supplied final keyframe with a gentle final hold. Leave readable
negative space on the left. The last view must be stable and suitable as a poster.

Silent. Landscape 16:9, minimum 1080p. No titles, no words, no letters, no logos,
no watermarks, no hands or people, no particles, no orbital rings, no neon grid,
no extra objects, no changing device design, no abrupt transitions. Motion must
remain clean when played backward, as the website will scrub this film by scroll.
