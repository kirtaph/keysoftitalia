Finali consegnati e controllati il 10 ottobre 2026.

## Valutazione dei finali

Entrambi utilizzabili. Stesso telefono grafite e stesso schema della crepa del
capitolo 03 (più fitta nell'angolo in basso a destra), stessa diagonale. Due soli
livelli: il vetro rotto sospeso sopra il corpo, che mostra batteria e pochi
componenti ordinati; sottile luce ambrata nello spazio fra i due. Niente mani,
niente volti; notebook e camicia sfocati sullo sfondo, banco e luce invariati.

- Desktop: il vetro è leggermente inclinato verso la camera e un po' arretrato
  rispetto al corpo; leggibile e credibile, non serve rigenerare.
- Mobile: la parte alta (notebook e camicia) è piena: il testo andrà su un velo
  scuro, come nei capitoli 02 e 03.
- La camera passa da altezza del banco a circa 45° dall'alto: il movimento del
  video deve salire e avvicinarsi in modo deciso.

## Prompt Veo — desktop 16:9

```text
Create an 8-second landscape 16:9 cinematic continuous shot connecting the
supplied FIRST FRAME and LAST FRAME, in one physical environment.

Start exactly from the supplied first image: a cracked graphite smartphone on
the warm stone counter, the customer's hands beside it, the notebook and the
white-shirted man softly blurred behind. Begin moving immediately.

From 0 to 1.5 seconds: the customer's two hands slide gently back and leave the
frame to the right. The phone does not move.
From 0.5 to 4.5 seconds: the camera rises and moves closer in one smooth arc,
until it looks down at the phone from about 45 degrees; the phone grows to
become the large central subject and the background drifts out of focus.
From 4.5 to 6.8 seconds: the cracked front display module lifts slowly and
straight up from the phone body by a few centimetres, staying aligned and
nearly parallel. As the gap opens, a thin warm amber light glows inside it and
reveals a neat, dark interior with the battery and a few components.
From 6.8 to 8 seconds: everything settles into the exact supplied final frame
and holds.

The SAME phone with the SAME crack pattern throughout; the glass stays cracked.
Only two layers: display and body. No other part moves, nothing explodes, no
screws, cables or particles. No hands after they leave, no tools, no face, no
extra phones, no text, no logos, no watermark, no cuts, no morphing, no dissolves.
Same counter, notebook, shelves and warm light. Premium tactile product
cinematography, controlled and calm, realistic shadows and reflections.
```
