due clip indipendenti di 8 secondi, con primo e ultimo frame allegati.

## Valutazione dei finali

Il desktop mostra un dettaglio molto ravvicinato dello schermo, conservando
cornici laterali e prospettiva obliqua. Il movimento deve arrivare a questa
angolazione: non forzare una vista perfettamente frontale.
Il mobile termina sul dettaglio ambrato a pieno campo, senza cornici visibili:
è un ritaglio ravvicinato del display orizzontale, non un notebook verticale.
Palette e soggetto sono coerenti con il capitolo 01. La continuità esatta delle
forme della schermata dovrà essere valutata nel filmato, durante l'avvicinamento.

## Prompt Veo — desktop 16:9

```text
Create an 8-second landscape 16:9 cinematic continuous shot connecting the
supplied FIRST FRAME and LAST FRAME. One action only: a bold physical camera
dolly towards the display of the same intact, stationary notebook.

Start exactly from the supplied first image: the notebook is on the warm
counter, with the original person behind it. Begin moving immediately.
Accelerate smoothly in the first 0.4 seconds into a decisive forward move.
From 0.4 to 2.5 seconds, approach the notebook screen and adjust camera height
and direction gently towards its central region. From 2.5 to 6.5 seconds,
continue the strong approach: the display grows dramatically in the frame,
with visible natural parallax between the counter, notebook and background.
From 6.5 to 7.6 seconds, decelerate smoothly into the supplied final framing.
Hold that exact end view briefly until 8 seconds. No long stationary opening
and no weak, barely noticeable zoom.

Match the oblique screen perspective of the supplied last frame. Retain
the narrow side bezel strips and any remaining warm background at the edges.
Do not force a frontal screen view. Stay physically in front of the glass.
The keyboard, counter and person leave the crop naturally as the camera
gets closer; they do not dissolve, slide away or transform.

Keep the notebook completely assembled, stationary and rigid throughout.
The lid angle, screen proportions, bezel and metal finish remain stable.
Keep the SAME static amber abstract dune-like wallpaper on the display.
Its existing shapes grow and crop naturally with the camera approach;
the wallpaper itself must not animate, morph or switch to another image.
Preserve warm lighting and subtle plausible glass reflections.

One continuous optical movement, premium controlled product cinematography,
strong readable change of scale, clean detail, smooth acceleration and braking.
No cuts, fades, black frames, portals, screen penetration, disassembly,
floating objects, new props, face reflections, generated text, logos,
watermarks, software interfaces, neon effects or particles. Silent video:
no dialogue, music or sound effects.
```

## Prompt Veo — mobile 9:16

```text
Create an 8-second portrait 9:16 cinematic continuous shot connecting the
supplied FIRST FRAME and LAST FRAME. One action only: a bold physical camera
approach to the display of the same intact, stationary landscape notebook.

Begin exactly on the supplied first frame. Start camera movement immediately
and accelerate smoothly during the first 0.4 seconds. From 0.4 to 2.5 seconds,
move decisively towards the central region of the notebook display, gently
adjusting the viewing direction. From 2.5 to 6.5 seconds, continue advancing
with a dramatic visible change of scale. The screen grows beyond the portrait
crop until all bezel edges leave the field of view. From 6.5 to 7.6 seconds,
brake smoothly and match the supplied final close-up of the amber screen
content. Hold that exact end view briefly until 8 seconds. The camera move
must feel substantial and cinematic, never a tiny zoom or a long static hold.

The notebook remains a LANDSCAPE device with its original dimensions and
fixed lid angle. The vertical camera naturally crops the sides of its screen.
Never stretch, narrow, rotate or reshape the notebook into a portrait device.
Keep the notebook stationary and fully assembled. The original person,
counter and keyboard leave the camera crop through the close approach,
without fading, sliding independently or morphing. Maintain physical parallax
and plausible perspective. Stay in front of the screen glass; no portal.

Preserve the SAME static amber abstract dune-like wallpaper from the start.
The displayed shapes are enlarged and naturally cropped by the approach;
they must not animate, melt or transform into a new wallpaper. Finish with
the screen content filling the entire portrait view as in the supplied end
image. Keep warm lighting and restrained realistic reflections throughout.

One smooth continuous shot, sharp tactile product cinematography, pronounced
forward motion with controlled acceleration and braking. No cuts, fades,
black frames, disassembly, floating components, new props, reflected faces,
generated words, logos, watermarks, software dashboards, particles, holograms
or neon effects. No black bars. Silent video, no speech, music or sound effects.
```
